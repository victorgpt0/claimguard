Statutory basis

Social Health Insurance Act, No. 16 of 2023. Recording an action here is an internal recommendation, not real enforcement.

  • s 48(5) — a provider that knowingly alters or falsifies information to defraud the Authority is liable to a fine (at most KES 2 million), suspension, or removal from the register of empanelled and contracted providers.
  • s 48(6)–(8) — suspensions and removals are gazetted; the facility receives no benefit from the Fund while suspended.
  • s 49 — unlawfully obtained sums are recoverable, and recovered money goes back into the Fund.
  • Practice — SHA has surcharged facilities to recover paid funds, withdrawn clinicians' platform access, referred cases to the DCI for prosecution, and asked the KMPDC / Clinical Officers Council to open disciplinary proceedings.
